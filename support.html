Common questions

Which PDFs work best? Digital (not scanned) PDFs of books, especially programming books. Scanned, image-only PDFs are not supported yet.

A page looks wrong. Open the chapter, choose "View Original Page", then "Report a problem" and send the report by email. It contains only that page, never the whole PDF.

How do I restore Reflow Pro on a new device? Open Settings › Reflow Pro › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing members get Pro too.

Do my notes sync? With Reflow Pro you can turn on iCloud notes sync in Settings. Books themselves stay on each device; import the same PDF on the other device and its notes appear.
