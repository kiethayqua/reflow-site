Reflow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Reflow turns PDF books into readable articles. It is built so that your books and notes stay yours. Reflow has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising, and the developer collects no data about you.

Your books

PDFs you import are converted entirely on your device. The PDF, the converted chapters, your reading position and your search history are stored only on that device and are never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Highlights and notes

Your highlights and notes are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud notes sync (off by default, Reflow Pro), they are stored in the private database of your own iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit, so they can appear on your other devices. That data is handled by Apple under your iCloud account and Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot read it. Turning sync off stops syncing; you can delete your notes in the app at any time.

Problem reports

"Report a problem" on an original page prepares an image of that one page with the layout blocks Reflow detected, plus a short technical summary (app version, iOS version, the detected blocks). It never includes the PDF itself. Nothing is sent automatically: the report goes only where you choose in the share sheet. If you send it to the developer, it is used only to fix the problem and is deleted once it is no longer needed.

Purchases

Reflow Pro is purchased through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer receives no payment details or personal information. The app checks your purchase on the device using StoreKit.

Photos

Reflow asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you choose to save an image. It never reads your photo library.

Children

Reflow does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
